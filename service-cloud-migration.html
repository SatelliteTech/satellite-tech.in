Modernization & Migration

Cloud Migration & Enablement

Migrate legacy on-premises servers, data silos, and complex monoliths to modern cloud environments. We design phased execution paths that eliminate risk, preserve business continuity, and train your internal engineering teams for autonomous operations.

Key Migration Specializations

Workload & Dependency Mapping

Automated telemetry discovery to expose hidden database calls, legacy cron tasks, and network dependencies before crafting the migration wave plan.

Database & Storage Migration

Zero-loss continuous replication from Oracle, SQL Server, MySQL, and PostgreSQL into cloud-native engines like AWS Aurora, RDS, and Cloud SQL.

Landing Zone Architecture

Pre-configured multi-account frameworks (AWS Control Tower, Landing Zones) with hardened guardrails, centralized billing, and federated SSO.

Staff Enablement & Runbooks

Hands-on knowledge transfer, operational runbooks, and incident triage workshops ensuring your engineers confidently operate the new stack.
